Dear Ducatista,
thank you for trusting us with the purchase of your new Desmo450 MX.
We recommend that you read the use and maintenance manual carefully , to quickly get familiar with your Ducati
and make the most of all its features . In the manual, we provide lots of useful advice and information on your safety ,
on how to take care of your bike and on how to maintain its value through correct maintenance by specialist Service
Centres.
You can also find this manual in digital format, always up-to-date, in the dedicated area of the Ducati website and in
the MyDucati App , which can be consulted both from a PC and a phone.
In this way, you will always have the most up-to-date version of the manual available and you will also find
information and frequently asked questions regarding your bike and the world of Ducati.
You can send suggestions for improvement regarding the contents of this Use and maintenance manual to the
following address: OwnerManual@ducati.com
This manual forms an integral part of the motorcycle and must be kept with it for its whole service life. If the
motorcycle is resold, the manual must always be handed over to the new owner. The quality standards and safety of
Ducati motorcycles are steadily improved as new design solutions, equipment and accessories are developed. While
the information contained in this manual is current at the time of going to print, Ducati Motor Holding S.p.A. reserves
the right to make changes at any time without notice and without any obligations. For this reason, the illustrations in
this manual might differ from your motorcycle.

Manual of your motorbike.

Ducati Quick Shift (DQS)
The Ducati Quick Shift (DQS) system allows the rider to upshift without using the clutch. The system does not work
when downshifting.
The DQS function is available from the 1st gear.
The DQS can be activated or deactivated using the dedicated button (A) regardless of the selected Riding Mode.
The DQS status is indicated by the dedicated blue LED (1). It will be steady on if the system is functioning properly or
it will flash if it is unavailable or malfunctioning.
A
1  |  1
Fig 29
The DQS is managed by the engine control unit, which detects lever actuation via the selector drum position sensor.
Here below are some tips that will ensure you properly exploit this feature:
• The Ducati Quick Shift only shifts up when torque is requested by opening the throttle control.
• The Ducati Quick Shift takes the same shift lever operation as with vehicle not equipped with the Ducati Quick
Shift.
• The Ducati Quick Shift is not designed for shifting automatically.
• For any gear change, the rider must push the gear lever up from its rest position for the entire lever travel and hold
it in this position until the gear shifting is complete.
• Once the gear shifting has been completed, the lever has to be fully released in order to allow another gearshift
acted by Ducati Quick Shift.
• If the rider does not move the shift lever up to end stroke during a Ducati Quick Shift request, gears may not be
fully engaged.
Attention
The Ducati Quick Shifter is designed to operate at engine speeds above 4000 rpm, therefore it is not possible to
change gear using DQS below this threshold.
Attention
While riding, the boot must not interfere with the rest position of the gearchange lever.
If the boot continuously touches the gearchange lever, the DQS system may malfunction when DQS is activated.
For use of the gearbox and clutch with DQS deactivated, refer to the “Gear change pedal” chapter.

Falldown detection
This system stops the engine in the event of a motorcycle fall. It is activated when a position similar to a fall is
detected for a certain period of time.
Among other things, one purpose of the system is to limit possible damage to the engine due to lack of lubrication.
To restart the engine after the system has been activated, provided there are no other technical problems, simply
return the motorcycle to an upright position and press the start button.
Attention

When the starter button is pressed, the EFI warning light will illuminate briefly to indicate that everything is working
properly.
If there are no errors, the warning light will remain off.
If there are errors, the EFI warning light will flash to indicate the error code detected using two types of flashes:
• One long flash means: +10
• One short flash means: +1
For example, when one long flash is followed by two short flashes, the EFI value is 12 (one long flash, equivalent to +10
flashes, plus two short flashes: +1 +1).
If there are multiple fault codes, the EFI warning light flashes to indicate them from the lowest to the highest number
for several cycles, interrupted by a pause without flashing.
The flashes are repeated continuously while the engine is running and after it is turned off, until the power supply to
the engine control unit is interrupted.
