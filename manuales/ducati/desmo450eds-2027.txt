Dear Ducatista,
thank you for trusting us with the purchase of your new Desmo450 EDS.
We recommend that you read the use and maintenance manual carefully , to quickly get familiar with your Ducati
and make the most of all its features . In the manual, we provide lots of useful advice and information on your safety ,
on how to take care of your bike and on how to maintain its value through correct maintenance by specialist Service
Centres.
You can also find this manual in digital format, always up-to-date, in the dedicated area of the Ducati website and in
the MyDucati App , which can be consulted both from a PC and a phone.
In this way, you will always have the most up-to-date version of the manual available and you will also find
information and frequently asked questions regarding your bike and the world of Ducati.
You can send suggestions for improvement regarding the contents of this Use and maintenance manual to the
following address: OwnerManual@ducati.com
This manual forms an integral part of the motorcycle and must be kept with it for its whole service life. If the
motorcycle is resold, the manual must always be handed over to the new owner. The quality standards and safety of
Ducati motorcycles are steadily improved as new design solutions, equipment and accessories are developed. While
the information contained in this manual is current at the time of going to print, Ducati Motor Holding S.p.A. reserves
the right to make changes at any time without notice and without any obligations. For this reason, the illustrations in
this manual might differ from your motorcycle.

news regarding its functions and features.

motorbikes are constantly updated. Check on the Ducati website the functions and features in the updated Owner's
Manual of your motorbike.

Software updates
Software updates
Some components of the motorbike are operated by
or involve the use of software. Such software may be
subject to or require updates.
• Any updates that may be necessary to ensure the
safety of the motorbike will be communicated by
Ducati and made available for installation at the
Ducati Service network.
• Information on updates that may be necessary
to maintain the conformity of the motorbike is
published on the Ducati website and the updates
are made available, for two years from the date
of purchase of the motorbike or for the longer
term of the conventional warranty (if active for the
motorbike), for installation at the Ducati Service
network.
• Further updates and new versions of the software
will be made available, in compliance with the
motorbike maintenance schedule indicated in this
Owner's Manual, for installation at the Ducati
Service network when the motorbike is serviced.
We invite you to periodically consult the section of the
Ducati website dedicated to updates and to download
and install the My Ducati App to keep informed of
available updates.
Attention
In order to maintain the motorbike's legal and,
if applicable, conventional warranty of conformity (if
applicable), you are required to install the updates
made available as soon as possible and, in any case,
within a reasonable period of time, also taking into
account the importance of the update.
If the updates are not installed within a reasonable
period of time, Ducati shall not be liable for any
conformity or safety defects deriving from the failure to
install the update.

Prevention of accidental burns and
thermal comfort management when
riding
During riding or immediately after use, it is normal to
feel heat coming from the engine, exhaust and cooling
system. The level of heat perceived may vary depending
on ambient temperature, riding style and clothing.
To improve comfort in high ambient temperatures
or, for example, in heavy traffic conditions, it is
recommended to:
• wear technical protective clothing for the legs, feet
and ankles;
• if thermal discomfort occurs, change your seating
position or take a short break;
• keep the engine speed at medium to low revs;
• switch off the engine during stops, whenever
possible.
Certain medical conditions (e.g. peripheral neuropathy,
impaired thermal sensitivity, diabetes or other physical
disorders) may reduce heat perception. In such cases
there is a risk of burns, as heat may not be fully
perceived.
This also applies when using heated seat or heated
handgrips. Therefore, particular care must be taken
when these systems are used for prolonged periods,
even at low ambient temperatures.

position of the control units on the vehicle (this affects the calculation of the vehicle's position and the level of
vibration on the components) could adversely affect the functionality of the electronic controls with consequences for
safety!

rider control the motorcycle, making it as easy to ride as possible. The presence of an active safety system should not
encourage the rider to ride at speeds and accelerations beyond the reasonable limits, not in accordance with the road
conditions, the laws of physics, good riding standards and the requirements of the road traffic code.
The DTC can be activated or deactivated using the dedicated button (A) regardless of the selected Riding Mode.
The DTC status is indicated by the dedicated orange LED (1). It will be steady on if the system is functioning properly

The Ducati Quick Shift (DQS) system allows the rider to upshift without using the clutch. The system does not work
when downshifting.
The DQS function is available from the 1st gear.
The DQS can be activated or deactivated using the dedicated button (A) regardless of the selected Riding Mode.
The DQS status is indicated by the dedicated blue LED (1). It will be steady on if the system is functioning properly or
it will flash if it is unavailable or malfunctioning.
A
1  |  1
Fig 31
The DQS is managed by the engine control unit, which detects lever actuation via the selector drum position sensor.
Here below are some tips that will ensure you properly exploit this feature:
• The Ducati Quick Shift only shifts up when torque is requested by opening the throttle control.
• The Ducati Quick Shift takes the same shift lever operation as with vehicle not equipped with the Ducati Quick
Shift.
• The Ducati Quick Shift is not designed for shifting automatically.
• For any gear change, the rider must push the gear lever up from its rest position for the entire lever travel and hold
it in this position until the gear shifting is complete.
32
• Once the gear shifting has been completed, the lever has to be fully released in order to allow another gearshift
acted by Ducati Quick Shift.
• If the rider does not move the shift lever up to end stroke during a Ducati Quick Shift request, gears may not be
fully engaged.
Attention
The Ducati Quick Shifter is designed to operate at engine speeds above 4000 rpm, therefore it is not possible to
change gear using DQS below this threshold.
Attention
While riding, the boot must not interfere with the rest position of the gearchange lever.
If the boot continuously touches the gearchange lever, the DQS system may malfunction when DQS is activated.

Falldown detection
This system stops the engine in the event of a motorcycle fall. It is activated when a position similar to a fall is
detected for a certain period of time.
Among other things, one purpose of the system is to limit possible damage to the engine due to lack of lubrication.
To restart the engine after the system has been activated, provided there are no other technical problems, simply
return the motorcycle to an upright position and press the start button.
Attention
Do not change the position of the motorcycle's electronic control units.
If the position of the control units is changed, the system may not be able to accurately detect the fall position.

• Oil level;
• Any oil, coolant or fuel leaks;
• Visually check for any loose engine or vehicle parts;
• Throttle play;
• Drive chain free play.
3. During the last 20 minutes of running-in (for a total

• Ride the motorcycle for the remaining 20 minutes,
avoiding high engine speeds but also avoiding
excessively low speeds in high gears, without

your safety!).

carry out the first service with all the checks listed (see
